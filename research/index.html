Our work runs from data to design. We first bring scattered biomedical data into integrated knowledge graphs and learned representations, then use those representations to predict what proteins do and which compounds act on them, and finally to generate new molecules and proteins that are tested experimentally with our collaborators. The five directions below describe this in more detail; the software is listed on the Software page and the full list of papers on the Publications page.
