--- title: Join description: Information for students and researchers interested in joining the Hacettepe Biological Data Science Lab. ---

The lab welcomes motivated students and researchers interested in machine learning for biology and chemistry, including biomedical knowledge graphs, protein function prediction, drug discovery and generative molecule and protein design. We are an interdisciplinary group: computer scientists and life scientists work together on the same projects. See Research and Publications for what we work on.

Undergraduate students

Students in Hacettepe's Computer Engineering and AI Engineering programmes can get involved in the lab's research projects. If you would like to work with us, send an email as described below.

Graduate students (MSc/PhD)

Graduate study in the lab is through Hacettepe University's graduate programmes, in the Department of Computer Engineering or the Department of Bioinformatics. Admission follows the university's application process; we recommend contacting Prof. Doğan before you apply.

Postdocs and visiting researchers

If you are interested in a postdoctoral or visiting position, please get in touch to discuss possibilities, for example through fellowship programmes.

How to apply

Email Prof. Doğan at {{ site.lab.pi_email }} with:

  • a short note on your research interests and which of the lab's projects or papers interest you;
