Research

Three questions we keep coming back to

  1. 01

    How do we connect scattered biomedical data?

    We integrate genes, proteins, pathways, diseases and drugs from open resources into knowledge graphs, and learn representations that can be queried and reasoned over.

    CROssBARv2CROssBAR

  2. 02

    What does a protein do, and what if we change it?

    We predict protein functions and the effects of variants with protein language models and graph learning, and design new protein sequences and backbones for a given function.

    ProtGENContVAR

  3. 03

    Can we design the molecule we need?

    We build generative models that design target-specific drug candidates, and predictive models of drug–target interactions and drug response. We test the best designs in the wet lab with our collaborators.

    DrugGENDeepResponse

Featured · Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025

DrugGEN: designing target-specific drug candidates with graph transformers

DrugGEN couples a generative adversarial network with graph transformers to design new molecules for a chosen protein target. For AKT1, five designs were synthesised; two inhibited the target significantly in vitro.
