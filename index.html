Welcome to the Biological Data Science Lab

We build machine learning models that use complex biological and biomedical data to learn how molecular sequence, structure and interactions shape function, and we apply these models to make testable predictions and design new molecules.

Our research sits where computer science meets the life sciences. We integrate heterogeneous biomedical data into knowledge graphs, learn representations of molecules, proteins and genes, predict their functions and interactions, and design new drug candidates and proteins with generative models. Along the way we build foundation models, language-model interfaces to biological data, and tools that others can reuse.

The lab is led by Prof. Tunca Doğan in the Department of Computer Engineering at Hacettepe University, Ankara. We release our code, data and models openly.
