Prof. Tunca Doğan has joined the Editorial Board of *Bioinformatics* (Oxford University Press), the official journal of the International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB), as an Associate Editor.

He will handle manuscripts in the journal's Sequence Analysis, Data and Text Mining, and Databases and Ontologies sections, focusing on AI and machine learning for bioinformatics: foundation models, representation learning and generative AI; biological data integration and knowledge graphs; and applications in drug discovery and protein function prediction.

* Journal: [Bioinformatics](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics)
