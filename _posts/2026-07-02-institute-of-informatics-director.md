Prof. Tunca Doğan has taken office as Director of the Hacettepe University Institute of Informatics.

The institute runs graduate programmes in areas including information systems, artificial intelligence, information security, computer graphics and health informatics. Colleagues from academia and industry interested in collaborating with the institute, and prospective students interested in these programmes, are welcome to get in touch.
