We are looking for one undergraduate scholar to join our TÜBİTAK ARDEB 1001 project **"High-Precision Protein Function Prediction with Graph Contrastive Representation Learning"** (project no. 124E639) through the TÜBİTAK 2247-C STAR programme.

**Who can apply:** undergraduate students in Computer Engineering, Artificial Intelligence Engineering, Data Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, or Industrial Engineering.

**How to apply:** applications are made through the TÜBİTAK Management Information System (TYBS) between 2 November and 16 November 2026, 17:30. Search for the project by its number, 124E639. The programme call is on the TÜBİTAK BİDEB website. Please upload your letter of intent and current CV merged into a single file.

We encourage students who want research experience in protein bioinformatics, graph-based deep learning and representation learning to apply.
