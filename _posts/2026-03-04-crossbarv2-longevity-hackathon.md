Atabey Ünlü and his teammates (team DeltaWave) won a 24-hour hackathon on extracting longevity-intervention data from the scientific literature. The event was supported by Nucleate Germany, JUNI and Google DeepMind.

The team built its solution on our CROssBARv2 biomedical knowledge graph, which they credited as a key component. Congratulations to Atabey and the team!

* CROssBARv2 knowledge graph: [GitHub](https://github.com/HUBioDataLab/CROssBARv2-KG/)
