Five student groups who carried out their graduation projects in our lab presented their work at the 2026 Project Exhibition of Hacettepe University's Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence Engineering departments. Each project is the result of a year of work:

* **BioAgents: Multi-Agent Language Model System for Computational Biology.** Team: Mert Ergün, Arya Zeynep Mete. Mentors: Atabey Ünlü, Bünyamin Şen.
* **ContVAR: Contrastive Graph Representation Learning for Protein Variant Function Prediction.** Team: Furkan Dursun, Boran Çakıl, Enes Karaarslan, Caner Ayvaz. Mentors: Erva Ulusoy, Bünyamin Şen.
* **MIND: Molecular Intelligence for Novel Discovery.** Team: Melih Koç, Arın Dalahmetoğlu, Can Ertaş, Mehmet Ali Toy. Mentor: Gökay Yiğit.
* **MIPLM: Matrix-Informed Protein Language Models.** Team: Kutay Özçepne, Ethem Ateş, Görkem Uz. Mentor: Gökay Yiğit.
* **MUCHEM: Multimodal Chemical Language Model.** Team: Furkan Necati İnan, Ayşe Yaren T., Sahra Sarı, Yüksel Çağrı Arslan, Efe Aydınalp. Mentor: Bünyamin Şen.

Model weights, code repositories and preprints for these projects will be shared soon. Congratulations to all the students, and best wishes for their careers after graduation!
