Our lab took part in the 25th European Conference on Computational Biology (ECCB 2026), held in Geneva from 31 August to 4 September and hosted by the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, with one proceedings talk and eight posters.

### Proceedings paper (oral presentation)

* **SELFormerMM: Multimodal Molecular Representation Learning via SELFIES, Structure, Text, and Knowledge Graph Integration** (Advanced Modeling and Machine Learning session). Presented by Tunca Doğan on behalf of first author Erva Ulusoy, who could not attend: Switzerland, like many European countries, had not been accepting visa applications from Türkiye for more than six months. Several other lab members were unable to attend for the same reason.

### Posters

* **COMPAS:** Contrastive Multimodal Polypharmacology-Aware Multi-Target Drug–Target Interaction Screener (Gamze Hatlar Deprem)
* **BepiCon:** A Geometric Deep Learning Framework for Conformational B Cell Epitope Prediction (Bünyamin Şen)
* Joint-Embedding Predictive Representation Learning for Protein–Ligand Pose Generation (Atabey Ünlü)
* Target-Conditioned Molecule Generation using Protein and Chemical Language Models with Bioactivity-Guided Post-Training (Furkan Öztürk)
* Designing Novel Enzymes with a Transformer-Guided Generative AI Framework (Ufuk Tanrıverdi)
* **DEEPScreen++:** A Modular Image-Based Deep Learning Framework for Drug–Target Interaction Prediction
* Target-Specific De Novo Design of Drug Candidate Molecules with Graph-Transformer-Based Generative Adversarial Networks
* **CROssBARv2:** A Unified Biomedical Knowledge Graph for Heterogeneous Data Representation and LLM-Driven Exploration

Thanks to the ECCB 2026 organising committee, SIB, the session chairs, reviewers and volunteers, and to everyone who stopped by our posters. The team in Geneva: Bünyamin Şen, Gamze Hatlar Deprem, Atabey Ünlü, Ufuk Tanrıverdi, Furkan Öztürk and Ahmet Emir Şaşmazlar. Next stop: ISMB/ECCB 2027 in Copenhagen.
