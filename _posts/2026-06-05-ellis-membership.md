Prof. Tunca Doğan has joined ELLIS, the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems, as a member. ELLIS is a pan-European network of excellence in AI and machine learning research that aims to keep top-level basic research in Europe and turn it into economic and societal impact.

Researchers from Türkiye remain underrepresented in major European AI networks despite a growing research output. Increasing that representation is one goal of the membership, together with the longer-term aim of establishing an ELLIS Türkiye Unit that would bring ELLIS's collaboration infrastructure, PhD programmes and cross-border research opportunities to Türkiye.
