The new release of OmniPath, together with the OmniPath Explorer web app, has been published in *Nucleic Acids Research*. The project is led by Dénes Türei and the Saez-Rodriguez group at Heidelberg University and EMBL-EBI, and our lab took part as a project partner.

OmniPath brings together more than 160 literature-curated and predicted resources into a single prior-knowledge hub for systems biology and bioinformatics: signalling and gene-regulatory networks, miRNA interactions, enzyme–PTM relationships, protein complexes, cell–cell communication, and functional and localisation annotations. OmniPath Explorer makes millions of annotations and hundreds of thousands of molecular interactions browsable on the web, and R, Python and Cytoscape clients connect it to existing analysis pipelines.
