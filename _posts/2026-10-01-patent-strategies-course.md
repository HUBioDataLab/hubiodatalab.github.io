Our alumna Dr. Fulya Çıray is teaching a new university elective at Hacettepe this semester, open to undergraduates from all departments: **SEC 643, Patent Strategies in Information Technologies**.

The course covers intellectual property (patents, copyright, trademarks and designs), the patentability of IT-based inventions, novelty and inventive step, claim drafting and amendment strategies, prior-art analysis and search reports, responses to examination reports, national and international filing strategies, and freedom-to-operate analysis and ethics. Students finish the semester by preparing and presenting their own sample patent application and strategic analysis.

Dr. Çıray worked for many years as a patent examiner at the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office (TÜRKPATENT) and as a Seconded National Expert at the European Patent Office in Munich. In her PhD at the METU Graduate School of Informatics, she used patent data to predict drug approvals with machine learning.

The course is taught in English on Fridays, 09:40–12:30, in Amphitheatre 2 of the Institute of Informatics building on the Beytepe Campus (3 credits / 4 ECTS, no prerequisites).
