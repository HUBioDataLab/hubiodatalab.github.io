Melike Akkaya presented our project "ProQuest: A Large Language Model Application on the UniProt Protein Sequence and Annotation Database" at ISCB-UK 2026, held at the University of Cambridge.

ProQuest lets researchers query the UniProt protein database in natural language. It combines three components: Solr query generation, ProtT5 embedding-based semantic sequence search, and a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline for biomedical question answering. Together they lower the technical barrier to protein data retrieval and speed up sequence similarity search compared with BLAST. The preprint and the tool will be released soon.
