Prof. Tunca Doğan has received the Hacettepe University 2025 Science Award in Science and Engineering. The award was presented at the ceremony marking the university's 60th anniversary and the opening of the 2026–27 academic year.

The award recognises the lab's long-running work in AI for science, including bioinformatics and cheminformatics, biomedical generative AI, biomolecular foundation models and biological knowledge graphs.

"Behind this award is the work of the whole team at the Hacettepe Biological Data Science Lab," said Prof. Doğan, thanking the lab's PhD, MSc and undergraduate students, collaborators in Türkiye and abroad, the funding agencies that support the lab's research, and Hacettepe University.
